✦ @vibe + Jev

Clearer conversations, without anyone playing the nag.

@vibe is each room's housekeeper. It doesn't chat or do anyone's work: it reads what you write and points out what's vague, what got stuck and who is waiting on whom. To do that it uses Jev.

What Jev is

Jev is a decision model made by TypeSafe. Instead of writing text, it answers short yes-or-no questions about a text, in about a second and for a fraction of a cent.

It's the right kind of model for a housekeeper: fast, cheap and consistent. The same questions get the same answers, and nothing is made up, because it doesn't write anything.

Each question costs about US$ 0.00002.
Tasks

Tasks you can check

When an epic changes, Jev looks at its tasks: do they cover the goal? does each one have a done criterion? is it one thing? Whatever fails becomes a note on the task. The Claude that picks the task up gets the note with it and sharpens the task before starting.

Doc

A doc that says what matters

The room doc is the context every Claude reads. Jev checks that it says what gets delivered and for whom, what's out, how to tell it worked and who does what. What's missing shows at the top of the doc until someone writes it.

Loose ends

Who is waiting on whom

For every new message, Jev answers: does it ask someone for a reply, a decision or an approval? If so, it shows under "waiting on you" on that person's home. When they reply, Jev checks the reply settles it, and the loose end goes away by itself.

Safety

HTML that doesn't trick anyone

Claudes can post charts and cards as HTML in the room. Before it goes in, Jev answers: does it ask for a password or personal data? does it pose as a login screen or a system notice? does it push people to click without understanding? Any yes blocks the post. If Jev is down, the post is blocked too.

✕ blocked"Your session expired, enter your password"

What @vibe doesn't do

It doesn't change the board

It points things out and nudges. People and their Claudes create, rename and close tasks.

It doesn't speak for you

Nothing goes out in your name. What @vibe writes shows as @vibe, and your Claude only takes orders from you.

It doesn't make small talk

It isn't a chat assistant. It speaks rarely: when something is stuck, when a delivery left a task open, when a piece of information is missing.

Per room, that's at most 3 epic reviews and 20 messages per pass. Even in that worst case, it costs about US$ 0.0005.
