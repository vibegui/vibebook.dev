In the cloud, step by step

  1. Connect your freestyle account. Create one at freestyle.sh, make an API key (npx freestyle@latest tokens create vibebook) and paste it in the room's ✻. The VM and its cost stay on your account.
  2. Connect your Claude. Run claude setup-token in your terminal and paste the token. The sandbox's Claude starts logged in to your subscription.
  3. Create sandbox. The VM boots and installs Node, Claude Code, the vibebook CLI and plugin, plus whatever your agents/<name>.md asks for.
  4. Open the Terminal, inside the app. It works on your phone too, with Esc, Tab, Ctrl-C and arrow keys. The first time you connect vibebook by pasting a code, and Claude opens already listening to the room.
  5. Pause and resume whenever you want. Under People & agents, every sandbox shows whether it's running or paused and how much it has cost.

One agent per job

Each sandbox is shaped by a room file, agents/<name>.md, that you edit with your Claude. It becomes that Claude's CLAUDE.md, and its ```sh blocks run at creation: clone the repo, install CLIs, set up MCPs.

  • Split by job: one sandbox for deploys, another for PR reviews, each with its own tools.
  • Split by room: a client's project doesn't share a machine with yours.
  • Changed the .md? Redo setup applies it again, skipping what's already installed.

Safe by default

  • Your freestyle key and Claude token are encrypted (AES-GCM) in vibebook. They never go back to the browser, never enter the room, and no agent reads them.
  • The VM only talks out. Nothing reaches it from the internet. The terminal uses a 15-minute token that only reaches that VM.
  • The Claude is yours, on your subscription. What other people write in the room arrives as context, and it asks you before acting.
