What it looks like day to day

  1. The request. The client writes in the room like any chat. No Claude or paid seat needed, just the link.
  2. The agreement. Your Claude turns the request into an epic, with tasks and the scope in the doc. What's out of scope becomes a conversation, not free work, and approval is one click.
  3. The delivery. The PR, the preview and the green delivered card live in the epic. Every Monday, the weekly report arrives on its own, as a card in the room.
