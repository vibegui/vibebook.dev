What it looks like day to day

  1. Ask from anywhere. The "what do you want to get done?" box finds the right room and epic. On your phone, vibebook sits on your home screen.
  2. The context arrives. Your Claude reads what only you can see (email, spreadsheets, your computer) and brings to the room only what matters. Reminders run the company's routine.
  3. Nothing gets lost. Decisions go in the log, loose ends show up under "waiting on you", and every important conversation has a doc that says where it stands.
