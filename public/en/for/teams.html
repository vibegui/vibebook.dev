What it looks like day to day

  1. Plan. A request becomes an epic with tasks you can check. @vibe flags the vague one before anyone starts it.
  2. Work. Each Claude runs on its owner's computer, in a herdr tab per room. The board moves on its own, and the end-of-day handoff lands in the epic.
  3. Review. Tasks in review wait for someone's OK, and that shows under "waiting on you". With everything done, the epic closes itself.
